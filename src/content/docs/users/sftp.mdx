SFTP gives you a fast, resumable connection for big transfers — uploading a
modpack, downloading a world, syncing with an external editor. For small edits,
the editor in the [Files tab](/users/files/) is faster.

## Prerequisites

- A server you can access, with `file.read` — the **SFTP** tab appears with the
  same access as the **Files** tab, so a write-only subuser never sees it.
- SFTP enabled on the node. If it is off, the tab says so.
- An SFTP client such as FileZilla, WinSCP, or Cyberduck.

## Connect

1. Open the server's **SFTP** tab. It shows **Host**, **Port**, **Username** and
   **Password**, each with a copy button, plus a **Quick Connect URI** you can
   paste into a client that understands `sftp://` URLs.
2. In your client, choose protocol **SFTP** — Catalyst does not offer plain FTP
   — and paste the host, port, username and password exactly as shown.
3. You land in your server's root directory, the same files as the **Files**
   tab. Uploads, deletes, renames and permission changes show up there as they
   happen.

Two details:

- The **username is the server's ID**, and the host and port belong to the
  **node** that runs the server.
- The **password is a temporary token, not your panel password.** The tab shows
  how long is left. Pick a **Token lifetime** and select **Rotate password** —
  or **Generate new** once it has expired — to issue a fresh one.

Every user gets their own credentials. If you are the owner, **Active SFTP
Sessions** lists each user's session with its expiry and a revoke button, plus
**Revoke all sessions**. Removing a user from the server revokes their SFTP
sessions immediately.

If the tab says *"SFTP is disabled on this server."*, SFTP is turned off for the
node — ask your administrator to enable it.

## Good practice

- **Stop the server** before uploading or replacing world data or databases.
- If your client warns about an unknown host key on first connect, verify the
  fingerprint with your administrator before accepting it.

## Troubleshooting

- **Authentication failed:** credentials rotate — copy them fresh from the SFTP
  tab instead of reusing a saved password.
- **Connection refused or timed out:** the node may block the SFTP port, or your
  network may block outbound SSH. See
  [networking issues](/troubleshooting/networking/).
- **Transfers stall on huge folders:** archive them first, upload one file, then
  extract it in the **Files** tab.
