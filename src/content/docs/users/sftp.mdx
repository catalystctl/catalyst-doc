SFTP gives you a fast, resumable connection for big transfers — uploading a
modpack, downloading a world, syncing with an external editor. For small edits,
the built-in editor in the [Files tab](/users/files/) is faster; reach for SFTP
when you are moving a lot of data.

Open the **SFTP** tab on your server to find your connection details.

## Connect

1. Open your server's **SFTP** tab. It shows **Host**, **Port**, **Username**,
   and **Password**, each with a copy button, plus a **Quick Connect URI** you
   can paste into a client that understands `sftp://` URLs.
2. In your SFTP client (FileZilla, WinSCP, Cyberduck):
   - Protocol: **SFTP** (not plain FTP — Catalyst does not offer insecure FTP).
   - Paste the host, port, username, and password exactly as shown.
3. You land in your server's root directory — the same files as the **Files**
   tab.

Two details worth knowing:

- **The username is your server's ID**, and the **host and port belong to the
  node** that runs your server.
- **The password is a temporary token, not your panel password.** It expires;
  the tab shows how long is left. Pick a **Token lifetime** and select **Rotate
  password** (or **Generate new** once it has expired) to issue a fresh one.

Every user gets their own credentials. If you are the owner, **Active SFTP
Sessions** lists each user's session with its expiry and a revoke button, plus
**Revoke all sessions**. Removing a user from the server revokes their SFTP
session immediately.

If the tab says *"SFTP is disabled on this server"*, SFTP has been turned off
for the node — ask your administrator to enable it.

## Good practice

- **Stop the server** before uploading or replacing world data or databases.
- Uploads go through the same permission checks as the file manager: you can
  only touch your own server.
- If your client warns about an unknown host key on first connect, verify the
  fingerprint with your administrator before accepting it.

## Troubleshooting

- **Authentication failed:** credentials rotate — copy them fresh from the SFTP
  tab instead of reusing a saved password.
- **Connection refused/timed out:** the node may block the SFTP port, or your
  network may block outbound SSH. See [networking issues](/troubleshooting/networking/).
- **Transfers stall on huge folders:** archive them first (zip), upload one file,
  then extract it in the **Files** tab.
