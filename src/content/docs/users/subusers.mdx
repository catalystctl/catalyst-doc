The **Users** tab adds subusers: extra accounts with limited access to your
server. Your friend logs in with their own account and only sees what you allow.

## Add a subuser

1. Open **Users** and select **Invite/Add User**.
2. Enter their panel account email (they need an account on the same panel).
3. Tick **only** the permissions they need:
   - A moderator might get console + power + files (view).
   - A builder might get files only.
   - Nobody needs everything except you.
4. Send the invite. Invites expire after 7 days — if your friend misses the
   window, remove the stale invite and send a fresh one. Once accepted, the
   server appears in their list.

## Least privilege, always

- Every permission is a way to break something. Console access can run
  destructive commands; file access can delete worlds; power access can stop
  the server mid-event.
- Remove subusers who no longer play. Stale access is the most common source
  of "someone griefed my server files" stories.
- Never share your own login instead — then nobody can tell who did what in
  [Activity](/users/activity/), and you cannot revoke access without changing
  your password.

## Remove access

Open **Users**, select the subuser, and remove them. Removal is immediate: open
sessions lose access. If you suspect misuse, check **Activity** first so you
know what changed.
